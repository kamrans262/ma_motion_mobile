1. Create the app

Open play.google.com/console with the Google account that should own MA Motion.

  1. Click Create app.
  2. App name: MA Motion
  3. Default language: English (United States)
  4. Choose App.
  5. Choose Free.
  6. Tick the policy boxes. The export-law box is fine: the app only uses normal HTTPS.
  7. Click Create app.

Play opens a dashboard of tasks. Work through sections 2, 3, and 4 next. Leave production alone.

2. Policy questions

In the left menu open Policy and programs, then App content.

Privacy policy

Stop here until you have a real web page. Play will not accept the app without a public privacy policy link. The app collects a name, email, password, and salon photo. Paste that page’s address when it exists. Do not type a fake link.

App access

Choose All or some functionality is restricted. Google’s reviewer has to be able to sign in.

Instructions to type:

Install the app. Sign in with the Maker demo account. The account should already have a finished Maker profile so discovery opens.

Leave the demo email and password blank until you have a real Maker login on the live API.

Ads

Answer No. This app does not show ads.

Content rating

Start the questionnaire. Pick the art and design category, not a game.

  • No to violence, sex, bad language, drugs, gambling, and crude humor.
  • Yes to people sharing images. Makers upload a salon photo, and other people can see artwork.

Submit it and apply the rating Play gives back.

Age

  • Target age: 18 and older only.
  • The app is not made for children.
  • Do not join Designed for Families.

News, government, money, health

Answer No to all of these. MA Motion is not a news app, a government app, a payments app, or a health app.

Data safety

The app sends account details to your own server over HTTPS. It does not sell data. It does not use data for ads.

NameCollected. Not shared. Used for the account.
EmailCollected. Not shared. Used for the account.
PasswordCollected. Not shared. Used only to register or sign in.
PhotosCollected. Not shared. This is the salon image from the photo library.
Location, crashes, how people tapNot collected in this version.
  • Encrypted in transit: Yes.
  • Can users ask for their data to be deleted: Yes, by emailing support. Do not say there is a delete button in the app. There isn’t one yet.

Full description

MA Motion is a place where art and design live. Join as a Maker. Create your account, write your profile, and choose a salon image from your photo library. Add your location, website, and contact email, then choose the types and styles that describe your work. When your profile is ready, discover artwork, search the collection, and filter by type, style, and location. This release is the Maker app. Sign in with a Maker account to use it.

6. Build the file Play wants

On your computer, in the project folder, run this. Replace the web address with the real API.

flutter build appbundle --release --dart-define=MA_API_BASE_URL=https://YOUR_API_HOST

Upload this file:

build\app\outputs\bundle\release\app-release.aab

If you leave out MA_API_BASE_URL, sign-in goes to a fake address and the reviewer cannot log in.
